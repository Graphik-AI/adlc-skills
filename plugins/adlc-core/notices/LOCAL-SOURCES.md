# Vecton original material and local additions

Vecton licenses its original package instructions, documentation, metadata,
maintenance tooling, independently authored frontend-testing-debugging skill,
and local additions under [MIT](Vecton-MIT.txt).

On 12 October 2026, the maintainer confirmed public redistribution rights for
locally supplied portions of `post-implementation` and `simplify` and selected
MIT for Vecton's original material. This records the maintainer's declaration;
it is not an independent reconstruction of earlier authorship.

The Anthropic-derived body of `simplify` remains under
[Apache-2.0](Anthropic-Apache-2.0.txt). Its modification notice remains in the skill.
Other imported material keeps its own license, attribution, and source identity.

The independently authored `frontend-testing-debugging` replacement contains no
imported OpenAI frontend source. No license claim is made for the excluded source.
See [the source notices](../NOTICES.md) for the full distribution boundary.
