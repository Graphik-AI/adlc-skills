# Grilling

Stress-test a consequential plan or decision through a focused interview. Reuse settled answers. Form a decision tree: ask only questions whose prerequisites are known, with a concrete recommendation and tradeoff. Batch a few independent questions if that helps; dependent questions wait for the answer.

Establish discoverable facts from the relevant source rather than asking the user to locate them. A bounded [research](../research/SKILL.md) task or supported subagent may help; continue independent work while its evidence is pending. Never fabricate the human's answer.

Ask through the host's available question tool or plain text. Keep the user's working style and cognitive load in mind. Clarify only missing choices that materially change behavior, scope, cost, safety or recovery. Once the consequential branches are settled, record the decision and proceed within existing authority. Do not require a second blanket confirmation; preserve any specifically requested approval pause.
